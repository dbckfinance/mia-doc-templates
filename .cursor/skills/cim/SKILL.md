# CIM Generation Methodology

## Structure

A CIM follows this mandatory structure:

1. **Disclaimer** - Confidentiality notice, no-reliance language
2. **Executive Summary** - 1-page deal overview
3. **Investment Highlights** - 3-5 key reasons to invest
4. **Company Overview** - History, management, operations
5. **Market Analysis** - TAM, SAM, growth drivers, competitive landscape
6. **Financial Performance** - 3Y historical + projections
7. **Valuation Indicators** - Multiples, DCF summary
8. **Risk Factors** - Key risks with mitigants
9. **Appendices** - Detailed financials, org chart, customer list

## Quality Standards

- All financial data must be sourced and dated
- Use €M for European deals, $M for US deals
- Include page numbers and section headers
- Professional typography: Inter font, M&IA brand colors
- Minimum 20 pages for a standard CIM

## Data Requirements

The agent must gather or receive:
- Company name, sector, geography
- 3 years of P&L (Revenue, EBITDA, Net Income minimum)
- Key operational KPIs
- Market size data
- Management team overview
