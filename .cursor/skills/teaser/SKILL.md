# Teaser Generation Methodology

## Formats

### Blind Teaser
- No company name, no identifiable details
- Sector and geography only at macro level
- Focus on financial metrics and growth story

### Named Teaser
- Full company identification
- Detailed metrics and positioning
- Sent after NDA signature

## Structure (1 page)

1. **Header** - Deal thesis headline
2. **Key Metrics Box** - Revenue, EBITDA, margin, growth
3. **Business Description** - 3-4 sentences
4. **Investment Highlights** - 3-5 bullets
5. **Transaction Overview** - Type, timeline, process
6. **Contact** - Advisor details

## Design Rules

- Single page, no scrolling
- M&IA brand colors and logo
- Clean layout with whitespace
- Metrics in a highlighted box
- Professional, not flashy
